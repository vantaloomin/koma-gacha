How to read the diagnostics

The reading-flow line connects panel centres in reading order. ▲ marks where the eye enters a panel and ○ where it leaves. A speaking panel reads entry → balloon → speaker's face → exit, and the gacha rewards cameras that put the balloon near the entry. Dashed ellipses are suggested balloon spots that stay clear of faces. The purple dashed line is the conversation axis: every camera stays on one side of it (the 180° rule), and a “!” on a score means a manual edit crossed it. Gaze arrows show where eyes point: ◎ means looking at the reader, × means facing away. Scores are green at 85+, amber at 70+, and red below that.

About Koma

Koma (コマ) is Japanese for a comic panel. Koma turns a page layout into a storyboard: cameras, poses and lettering for every panel.

Pages

Generates manga and comic page layouts in batches. Open a page to export it or storyboard it.

Shots

Gives every panel a camera that fits its shape and the scene's tone. Lock the panels you like and roll again for the rest. Uses mannequins or your own VRM models.

Poses

Edit, combine and save poses for one, two or three characters, with props.
